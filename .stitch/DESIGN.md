# Design System: LearningEnglish
**Project ID:** `16275227875844452464`

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
LearningEnglish uses a warm editorial system with the internal theme name **Linen & Coral**. The creative north star is **The Gentle Curator**: a calm, premium after-class learning companion for families rather than a noisy gamified kids app. The interface should feel like a curated page from a high-quality family learning magazine, with generous breathing room, rounded cards, and low-pressure feedback.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Warm Linen** (`#FFF8F5`): main page background and base canvas.
- **Soft Sheet** (`#FFF1E9`): grouped sections, lower surfaces, and soft content framing.
- **Paper White** (`#FFFFFF`): lifted cards, flashcards, and focused learning surfaces.
- **Coral Jam** (`#F28C6B`): primary call-to-action color and active highlight.
- **Cocoa Coral** (`#98462A`): deeper anchor for gradients, emphasis, and stronger button contrast.
- **Mint Leaf** (`#9DF3DF`): positive states, progress, and supportive learning feedback.
- **Forest Mint** (`#006B5C`): stronger success text or contrast accents.
- **Ink Cocoa** (`#251910`): primary text and major headings.
- **Dust Brown** (`#55433D`): secondary text and metadata.
- **Outline Variant** (`#DBC1B9`): low-contrast strokes, ghost borders, and subtle separators.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Display and navigation:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- **Body and Chinese UI copy:** `Be Vietnam Pro`.
- Headlines should feel editorial and calm, not playful in a toy-like way.
- Labels and chips can lean slightly friendlier, but must stay highly legible for parents.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped or very rounded, often using a subtle gradient from `Cocoa Coral` to `Coral Jam`.
- **Cards/Containers:** Rounded 20px+ corners with tonal layering instead of obvious borders.
- **Inputs/Forms:** Low-contrast filled surfaces with soft borders that become more visible on focus.
- **Flashcards:** Large rounded surfaces with centered typography and minimal distractions.
- **Insight modules:** Calm panels that feel editorial and trustworthy, never corporate.

## 5. Layout Principles
- Avoid hard divider lines whenever possible. Use tonal shifts and whitespace instead.
- Phone layouts stay single-focus and vertically paced.
- Tablet layouts use left navigation rails and split panes for list-detail and source-detail relationships.
- On larger screens, keep original worksheet content visible beside extracted knowledge or practice content.
