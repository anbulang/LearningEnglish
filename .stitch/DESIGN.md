# Design System: LearningEnglish
**Project ID:** `16275227875844452464`

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
LearningEnglish now uses a warmer, more expressive version of **Linen & Coral**. The creative north star is no longer only "The Gentle Curator", but a more vivid extension of it: **The Living Scrapbook**. The product should feel like a premium family learning picture book assembled from paper pieces, stickers, and illustrated lesson moments.

The interface must feel:
- warm and emotionally safe for young children
- trustworthy and readable for parents
- more lively than the first pass, but never noisy
- tactile, scrapbook-like, and illustration-led rather than SaaS-like

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Warm Linen** (`#FFF8F5`): main canvas and primary page background.
- **Soft Sheet** (`#FFF1E9`): grouped sections and quiet content framing.
- **Paper White** (`#FFFFFF`): lifted cards and reading surfaces.
- **Coral Jam** (`#F28C6B`): primary action, highlights, and friendly emphasis.
- **Cocoa Coral** (`#98462A`): stronger CTA anchor, contrast, and gradient depth.
- **Mint Leaf** (`#9DF3DF`): progress, positive feedback, and supportive accents.
- **Forest Mint** (`#006B5C`): stronger success contrast and labels.
- **Butter Yellow** (`#FFD86A`): reward badges, celebratory stickers, and illustration highlights.
- **Sky Blue** (`#BFE7FF`): listening, speaking, and audio-focused modules.
- **Ink Cocoa** (`#251910`): primary text.
- **Dust Brown** (`#55433D`): secondary text and metadata.
- **Outline Variant** (`#DBC1B9`): very soft fallback strokes when accessibility needs separation.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Display and navigation:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Body and Chinese UI copy:** `Be Vietnam Pro`
- Keep the typography premium and calm even when the visuals are playful.
- Let headings create stronger contrast through scale and spacing, not through loud color.
- Child-facing moments can feel softer and bigger, but parents must never lose scanability.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Hero modules:** Large AI-illustrated scenes are required on key screens such as home, upload, lesson detail, and report.
- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped or very rounded, often with gentle coral gradients and sticker-like emphasis.
- **Cards/Containers:** Layered paper-like surfaces with tonal separation, soft diffused shadows, and occasional collage overlap.
- **Tags and badges:** Sticker-like, slightly more expressive than before, used for encouragement, topic labels, and review state.
- **Learning modules:** Vocabulary, sentence, and review blocks should feel like pieces inside a learning pack, not plain list sections.
- **Information panels:** Tablet side panels should avoid generic white-card stacking and instead use visual anchors and stronger hierarchy.

## 5. Layout Principles
- Keep the original information architecture and task flow intact.
- Use illustrated anchors and layered shapes to create rhythm instead of adding more text.
- Avoid hard dividers whenever possible; prefer tonal transitions and spacing.
- Phone layouts remain single-focus and vertical.
- Tablet layouts retain rail and split-pane patterns, but the content area should feel editorial and illustrated rather than dashboard-like.

## 6. Image & Illustration Rules
- Every key screen must have at least one memorable visual anchor.
- Use AI-illustrated scenes with a soft picture-book tone: parent-child study, worksheet-to-review transformation, animal or object learning cards, growth/report metaphors.
- Prefer paper-cut, collage, sticker, and soft motif language over corporate vector illustration.
- Do not use hyper-realistic 3D art, glossy enterprise illustrations, or loud arcade-style gamification.
