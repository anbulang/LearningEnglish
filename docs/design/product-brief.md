# LearningEnglish Product Brief

## Product Definition
`LearningEnglish` is a parent-led English review app for early learners. It converts printed worksheets from live English classes into structured digital review packs with guided listening, speaking, lightweight practice, and parent coaching.

## Target Users
### Primary user: Parent
- Captures and uploads paper worksheets
- Organizes lessons by child, date, topic, and teacher
- Reviews AI-extracted words and sentence patterns
- Runs guided after-class practice with the child
- Tracks progress and weak points

### Secondary user: Child
- Reviews vocabulary with image and audio support
- Listens and repeats words and sentences
- Completes simple matching, choice, and speaking tasks
- Repeats practice in short sessions

## Core Problems
- Printed materials are easy to lose and hard to search later.
- Static worksheets do not become reusable, trackable review content by themselves.
- Parents want to help but often do not know what to say, what to review first, or how to pronounce correctly.

## Product Goals
1. Make each lesson easy to capture, archive, search, and revisit.
2. Automatically extract words, phrases, sentence patterns, dialogues, and topic summaries from uploaded materials.
3. Turn extracted content into child-friendly review tasks and parent-guided practice scripts.

## Version Strategy
### Phase 1: Foundational MVP
- Upload photos or scans
- OCR and English content extraction
- Lesson card generation
- Vocabulary and sentence extraction
- Review pack with word cards, listening, and simple games
- Parent coaching prompts
- Review history

### Phase 2: Guided AI Practice
- AI speaking partner
- Pronunciation scoring
- Adaptive review plans
- Multi-child support
- Weak-point tracking

### Phase 3: Personalized Learning System
- Adaptive difficulty
- Personal vocabulary bank
- Knowledge graph across lessons
- Periodic reports and recommendations

## Differentiators
- Built around the child's real classroom materials rather than generic textbook content
- Strong parent-assisted review mode instead of child-only independent learning
- Low-age-friendly interface with short sessions and low cognitive load

## Product Principles
- Child-first practice, parent-first control
- Encourage repetition, not test anxiety
- Keep every workflow short, obvious, and recoverable
- Always show the lesson source alongside generated knowledge and tasks

## Success Criteria
- A parent can create a lesson and get a usable review pack in under 5 minutes.
- A child can complete a 5-10 minute review flow without navigating multiple deep menus.
- A parent can identify what to review next from the home screen and weekly report.
